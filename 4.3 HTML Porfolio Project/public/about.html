About Me

My name is Hatefe, and I am passionate about technology and problem-solving. I have been living in the UK for more than three years and have pursued various IT and programming courses to build a career in the field. Despite facing language barriers as a refugee, I have worked hard to gain knowledge and improve my skills.

I have studied programming languages such as C++, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and HTML. My academic journey includes completing an Access to HE Computing and Programming course, where I achieved six distinctions out of eight graded modules. I am currently studying part-time at CodeYourFuture, a program that supports individuals transitioning into IT careers.

Previously, I worked as an online eBay shop manager, an Asylum Advice Worker, and a production team lead in my father's factory in Iran. Currently, I work part-time at Brushstrokes Community Project, assisting asylum seekers with their immigration issues and providing interpretation support in Farsi when needed.

My goal is to build a successful career in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science. I plan to study at the University of Birmingham, focusing on AI and software development. I am eager to continue learning, tackling new challenges, and contributing to the tech industry.
